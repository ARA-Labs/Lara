Verdict
The direction matches canonical M2b, but the current plan is not implementation-ready. Three planned cost statements are false, the proposed tightness family is not tied to M1 realizability, the hardness input encoding is incomplete, and the hardness/tractability fork is not exhaustive.

Accepted scope
1. Run the fixed-policy structure/reduction spike first.
2. Fix one canon, sigma, policy, and registry independent of source instances.
3. Define the non-grounded problem as a promise problem over an explicit encoded StructuredAF: credulous complete acceptance of an in-range argument, promised Realizable under the fixed context.
4. If neither a fixed-policy hardness reduction nor a tractability theorem is established, stop and keep #181 open; do not infer tractability from a failed kit.
5. Prove grounded attack-query complexity with the corrected universal floor n^2 and upper bound n^3(1+n).
6. Establish worst-case quartic tightness only with a family carrying an M1 Realization under the same fixed context.
7. Make Invariants.status canon F p the paper-citable grounded query.
8. Use a shared verified status procedure: compute grounded once, aggregate labels/support, and prove equality with Invariants.status.
9. Keep generic AF/Claim bounds internal.
10. Retain Lean agreement, AxCheck, negative evaluations, name-map, Runtime.hs correction, and the durable decision record.

Strongest challenges
- groundedC_cost_ge n^3 is false because defendedAuxC can stop after one carrier query; a sampled first-node-attacks-all family costs n^2.
- pinnedAF_cost_ge n^4 is false: counts 2107 < 2401 at n=7 and 3136 < 4096 at n=8.
- The proposed statusCC bound is false under its stated no-sharing schedule; one size-24/support-48 instance costs 30,978,584 queries against a 25,272,600 bound.
- A generic Grounded.AF witness does not establish tightness on the realizable class.
- Nat -> Nat -> Bool is not a finite classical input encoding; the reduction must count the explicit label vector, adjacency matrix, query, and all source-instance syntax.
- A generated policy per graph risks making the restricted class effectively unrestricted; fixed policy was selected.

- New Haskell query-count mirror, IORef counter, golden emitter, shell gate, Cabal wiring, and CI wiring.

- Runtime, RAM, or wall-clock bounds.
- NP-membership and complexity-class bookkeeping in Lean unless later needed to state the paper theorem precisely.
- Treating a failed realization kit as proof of tractability.
