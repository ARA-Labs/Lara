# Belief Hoare Logic: reference and artifact assessment
**Date:** 2026-10-07

## TL;DR

Lara will reconstruct Belief Hoare Logic (BHL) in Lean from Kawamoto, Sato and Suenaga's [arXiv v3 reference](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3), with documented corrections to its predicate, history and proof contracts. The author-linked StatWhy artifact provides useful examples and recorded foreign-prover sessions, but its semantics and axiom context do not meet Lara's Lean-kernel contract. The researcher approved proceeding with documented corrections and approved Mathlib revision `81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997` for actual real-valued probability semantics; the separate finite rational example remains required. Soundness, relative completeness, executable correspondence and evidence-sensitive artifact warrant remain distinct obligations, and approval establishes none of their unimplemented theorem claims.

The reconstruction retains every deliverable and permits typed outer quantification over actual value, world and finite-trace carriers instead of an impossible arbitrary-real-to-integer encoding. It preserves finite syntax and excludes quantification underneath `K`. PR 06 now constructs and proves the richer language's legal representation; this is not a proof of the original integer-tuple ELHT claim. Every correction remains visible in the affected theorem's hypotheses and source mapping. An unforeseen frozen-logic or numeric-dependency change still requires an issue #24 scope decision before cutover or any requirement reduction.

## Which model and numerical statements must be reconstructed?

The paper's [Definitions 1-4](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S4.SS2) use states `(memory, last action, history)` and nonempty finite traces, with empty history at the initial state. Observation includes the entire trace of observable memory, actions and test multisets. Thus hidden population parameters are excluded from visible memory while test accounting is observable. Equality of final memory is insufficient for accessibility. Distinct dataset identifiers are symbolic names; the reference history keys are actual equal dataset values, not fresh names or physical execution lineage.

The numeric contract realizes [Definition 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#Thmdefinition5) and [§6.2.2's event interpretation](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S6.SS2.SSS2) through genuine measurable carriers and probability measures. A test supplies a statistic, its declared null law, a tail relation and evidence that each observed tail event is measurable. Binding that law to the population under the null is an additional local mathematical condition. Calibration is proved for a selected test, not a primitive field promising all tests are sound. The union and intersection bounds below require the coupling's marginals, not statistical independence.

## Which assertion and belief statements are required?

The [ELHT grammar](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S6.SS1) separates logical integer variables from program variables and excludes quantification underneath knowledge. The corrected language permits typed outer ghost quantifiers over actual values, worlds and finite traces, while preserving finite syntax and the prohibition under `K`. A quantifier-free modal layer and outer assertion layer enforce that restriction. PR 03 proves scope and capture-avoiding substitution; PR 06 proves concrete finite representation and uniform satisfaction. This is expressiveness for the documented richer language, not validation of the paper's original integer-vector encoding or general research-representation adequacy.

The defining meaning is `K(φ ∨ τ ∨ ¬varpi)`, where `τ` combines the numerical exception with exact test history and `varpi` states the test/population requirements; see [§6.1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S6.SS1.SSS5) and [§6.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S6.SS3). Knowledge quantifies over every observation-equivalent world. Statistical belief can therefore hold when the alternative is false, through an exceptional sample or model failure. It supplies neither a posterior probability nor truth of the alternative. Lara's defeasible argument support remains a separate relation.

## What does the proved relative-completeness result require?

## What does a method assertion mean in a Lara artifact?

The artifact fragment has an independent meaning before any bridge checker accepts it. Following the [audited binding contract](theory-bhl-decision.md#what-exactly-does-the-independent-artifact-judgment-require), it retains the exact accepted declared/raw/checked source snapshot, claim identifier and interpretation, hypothesis/test bindings, statistical model, `P/C/Q`, derivation assumptions, initial/final worlds and execution, selected arguments and applicability dependencies, residual assumptions, and declared modeled-history coverage. Certified inputs also bind the evidence snapshot, manifest and registry. Equality of labels or identically spelled prose is insufficient: transport needs exact equality or a proved interpretation-preserving map for the actual carriers.

The reference contract distinguishes a procedure-wide conditional assertion from a single modeled application and from defeasible warrant at a source snapshot. It derives a postcondition only from actual precondition satisfaction and the exact run. Applicability evidence can support a conditional method claim while leaving assumptions unresolved. Natural-language interpretation fidelity, physical sampling fidelity and external-log completeness are obligations outside the checked model, as the paper's [§10](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S10) already distinguishes formal reasoning from external justification.

## Which scientific assumptions stay outside the proof?

A Lean proof establishes a statement inside the bound mathematical model. It does not show that the claim's prose faithfully describes the selected hypothesis or that the modeled population describes a physical population. The reference's [§10.1-10.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S10) separates statistical requirements from external empirical justification. Lara must retain that separation even when it has checked applicability evidence and a complete modeled history.

| Boundary | What the formal library can establish | What remains external or needs a separate theorem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim interpretation | Exact symbolic claim/hypothesis binding and preservation under an explicit interpretation map. | Fidelity of natural-language prose to that interpretation; identifiers alone do not prove it. |
| Sampling/model applicability | Conditional null-law/statistic and calibration proofs under the declared sampling/population conditions. | Physical i.i.d. sampling, normality or other real-population requirements; support for these claims is defeasible evidence, not mathematical satisfaction. |
| Test numerics | Exact finite rational computation/event equality and general measurable-event definitions; specific analytic results when proved. | Correctness of an external numerical implementation, approximation error and all unimplemented continuous-test contracts. |
| History coverage | Every modeled test appears with correct multiplicity and alias-safe accounting in the reference ledger. | Physical logging completeness, authenticity and omitted external tests; absence of a submitted entry cannot certify absence of the action. |
| Statistical interpretation | Knowledge of alternative/exception/model-failure disjunction under the stated world relation. | Truth of the alternative, posterior probability, or a procedure-wide frequentist error bound without its own strategy/distribution proof. |
| Research outcomes | Formal procedure, support, binding and revision distinctions. | Improved scientific outcomes, empirical efficacy and general representation adequacy; no empirical protocol is released by this workstream. |
