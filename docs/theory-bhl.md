# Belief Hoare Logic: contracts and theorem record

## TL;DR

Lara's Belief Hoare Logic (BHL) checks statistical methods under explicit assumptions and connects those methods to evidence-dependent claim support. The corrected Lean development includes soundness, relative completeness, a calibrated finite statistical instance, executable checking and source/evidence composition. This document owns the architectural rationale, frozen contracts, theorem assumptions, verification commands and [reference audit](#how-does-the-theory-map-to-the-reference).

## Why is BHL part of Lara?

A research artifact can report a result and support a claim without specifying whether its statistical procedure warrants that inference. BHL expresses that methodological meaning through preconditions, programs, epistemic postconditions and test histories. The reference is Kawamoto, Sato and Suenaga, [Sound and Relatively Complete Belief Hoare Logic for Statistical Hypothesis Testing Programs](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3). History sensitivity, statistical-belief semantics, soundness and relative completeness are prior results, not Lara novelty claims.

Lara uses BHL as its statistical-method component alongside argumentation and context semantics. Its artifact binding connects method judgments to claims, interpretations, evidence and source revisions. Merely packaging a method certificate beside an accepted argument would not establish how those meanings interact.

The baseline covers complete modeled histories, artifact binding, bounded evidence-sensitive revalidation and a partial-record witness. The general compatible-history calculus, profile-relative entitlement, ordered process histories, statistical validity over incomplete ledgers, broader revision and representation adequacy are the [research-process layer](theory-process.md), which builds on this baseline without changing it. Neither defines an empirical protocol.

## What are the architectural boundaries?

Neither statistical belief nor Lara support implies empirical truth. Support for an assumption cannot establish that assumption or knowledge of it in BHL. Residual assumptions remain visible even when evidence supports their applicability. Interpretation fidelity, physical sampling, capture adequacy and runtime refinement remain external obligations. Keeping them external limits what the checker establishes, but prevents a successful check from being reported as empirical truth.

BHL's observation-equivalence relation differs from Lara's accepted-context bridge relation. The former supports its epistemic laws; the latter generally lacks the stronger frame laws, as stated in the [possible-world theory](theory-pw.md#the-outer-frame-and-satisfaction). There is no modality coercion. Any future translation must state and prove its required conditions.

BHL does not alter core, admission, quarantine, evidence or public-status semantics. It adds no `.lara` syntax or wire changes, no physical history capture and no receipt service.

### What exactly does the independent artifact judgment require?

A method binding retains the exact accepted source snapshot, claim and hypothesis interpretation, model, precondition/program/postcondition, derivation premises, initial/final worlds and execution, selected support and applicability dependencies, residual assumptions and declared history coverage. Certified inputs also bind their evidence snapshot, manifest and registry. Identity means equality of actual carriers or a proved interpretation-preserving map, not equality of author-chosen labels.

| Judgment | Required evidence | Conclusion boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conditional method validity | A checked derivation with valid underlying premises and model side conditions | Every terminating modeled run from a precondition-satisfying world has the stated postcondition |
| Modeled application | Conditional validity, actual initial-world precondition satisfaction and the bound execution | The modeled final world satisfies the postcondition |
| Artifact warrant under residual assumptions | Exact accepted run, raw/checked typing, retention, grounded in-label and unblocked status for selected support and every named dependency | Selected supports remain eligible; independent `ArtifactMeaning` also requires claim association, conditional validity and applied-mode application, without proving residual assumptions true |

`ConditionalMethod` is independent triple validity. `ModeledApplication` supplies genuine precondition satisfaction and the exact execution; its postcondition is derived, not supplied as a certificate premise. `ArtifactWarrant` requires selected support and every named dependency to survive admission and checking and remain justified and unblocked. A conditional payload retains its assumptions without asserting a modeled application. The [implemented bridge contract](#which-source-and-claim-contracts-are-implemented) gives the exact source-facing declarations.

A claim identifier does not prove interpretation fidelity. A modeled execution does not prove that physical sampling occurred as modeled. Complete modeled history does not prove completeness of a submitted log.

### What does composition establish?

The bridge uses actual accepted source/evidence runs, preserves ordinary observations when method metadata is forgotten, and derives warrant loss or transport from source changes. These results are not obtained merely by projecting fields from two successful checks.

| Case | Established distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Quarantine of selected applicability evidence | An accepted source loses that dependent warrant while the conditional method theorem remains valid |
| Safe source update | An explicitly rebound warrant survives under binding, admission, support and attack-preservation hypotheses |
| Stale or incompatible certificate | A copied certificate cannot attest a different source snapshot, hypothesis interpretation, model or dependency binding |
| Supported applicability with a false assumption | Lara support cannot discharge a mathematical precondition or knowledge premise |
| Partial submitted record | Two compatible complete histories can disagree on the method conclusion despite the same nonempty submitted record |

Quarantine concerns a selected dependency witness; independent alternative support can preserve another warrant for the same claim. Source acceptance, public claim status, mathematical validity and applicability warrant remain distinct. The [revision results](#which-revision-conditions-derive-loss-or-transport) state sufficient rebinding conditions, not a theorem for every harmless edit. The [partial-record result](#what-does-the-nonempty-submitted-record-fail-to-determine) concerns a latest-test projection, not every partial record; the general compatible-completion calculus is the [research-process layer](theory-process.md).

## Which reference corrections define the local contract?

The [reference audit](#which-discrepancies-require-corrections) distinguishes published statements from the corrected proof contracts. Ordinary predicate meanings are rigid relations on explicit arguments, or require a proved preimage operation for their declared dependencies. Test execution has distinct syntax from pure assignment; statistical atoms take explicit dataset terms. This prevents assignment substitution from overlooking state changes hidden in predicate names.

History is a canonical finite multiset indexed by dataset values and test identities. Named history lookup derives counts from this ledger, so aliases share counts and reassignment changes lookup without changing history. Exact-history assertions compare complete value-indexed multisets, including repeated tests, rather than the paper's alias-inconsistent name-count conjunction.

Knowledge uses equality of complete observation traces, not just final memory and history. Admitted worlds support the prefix, extension and accessibility-lifting laws needed by substitution and parallel correspondence. Threshold monotonicity uses `≤`; equality-indexed belief retains its distinct, nonmonotone meaning.

The corrected assertion syntax separates fixed ghost worlds/traces from the ambient semantic view. Ambient terms expose memory, history, derived sampling provenance and compatible hidden alternatives, not raw action trace, last action or length. A fixed ghost retains its own trace, but comparison with the ambient world uses the full semantic view. Otherwise a ghost naming one schedule could distinguish independent schedules and invalidate `Par`. The [assertion laws](#which-assertion-and-statistical-belief-laws-are-checked) cover nested knowledge and unchanged ghost environments. Relative completeness uses typed finite assertion syntax, explicit local control/effect equations and reconstruction of actual prefix-extending execution; it neither assumes an opaque weakest-precondition atom nor defines execution as endpoint witnesses. The theorem remains relative to its stated assertion-theory premises.

## Which assertion and statistical-belief laws are checked?

The frozen syntax follows the corrected binder and modal contract, not unrestricted memory substitution. `GhostSort` distinguishes ordinary typed values, datasets, full worlds and finite traces. `BoundGhost context sort` indexes a binding intrinsically; outer `Assertion.all` changes the context. `ModalFormula` has no quantifier constructor, so no quantifier can be introduced beneath `K`. References to ghosts bound outside `K` remain legal and rigid across alternatives. Ghost terms are bound references or interpreted constants.

`AssertionTerm`, `ValueArguments` and `TestExpression` are finite symbolic syntax. Ordinary interpreted functions and predicates accept explicit ordinary value arguments only. The ambient term denotes a semantic view, never the raw current world, full trace, final action or trace length. A full-world or trace ghost may inspect its own fixed value. `atView` reevaluates its target at each modal node; it does not freeze the actual world's hidden memory under knowledge.

`AssertionInterpretation` supplies mathematical functions, hypotheses and test distributions. ## How does BHL execution work?

The execution modules implement the [source small-step relation](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S5.F1), terminating execution and noninterfering parallel correspondence.

`ProgramExpr`, `ProgramArgs` and `DatasetExpr` are finite intrinsically sorted source syntax. Reads and targets are observable; ordinary mathematical functions remain in the fixed interpretation, not the AST. `ProgramModelBinding` equates every model command result, including `none`, with its independently defined primitive effect. `ProgramContext.ofRelations` installs that equation while retaining independent initial and sampling relations. Computing a p-value is a pure expression; only the dedicated test command appends its actual dataset-value/test singleton to the canonical ledger.

`Step` uses `Option Program`: terminal `none` cannot step. Source skip appends a command state; false-loop exit takes one silent step without appending one. Sequence and parallel completion propagate the child's final step without another administrative transition. `FiniteLoopIterations` characterizes terminating loops using actual guard evaluations and body runs, without assuming termination or fairness. The finite residual graph contains all possible symbolic control edges, including loops and nested parallel; its local edge effects are equivalent to the reference step relation, not an opaque whole-execution predicate.

`SourceEffect` and `EffectOrigin` retain the actual source site, guard choice, evaluated write payload and ledger delta. `PairedSteps` is connected to reference `Steps` in both directions. Projection gives isolated runs of exactly `m` and `k` source steps with `n = m + k`; replay reconstructs the same effects and choices on read agreement. If the initial ledger is `H`, isolated ledgers are `H + δL` and `H + δR`, and the shared/sequential ledger is `H + δL + δR`, not their sum with `H` counted twice. Every terminating permitted interleaving has an actual same-count left-then-right sequential representative.

The representative agrees on visible memory/datasets, complete ledger, actual hidden memory, compatible-hidden set and sampling provenance. Different schedules retain different ordered action traces; they are not asserted to be raw `Accessible` endpoints. Hidden rebuilding and replay provide admitted epistemic forth/back alternatives. Satisfaction correspondence covers every legal assertion in the same arbitrary ghost context/environment, including nested knowledge, reference scopes and rigid world/trace ghosts. None of these operational results assumes scientific calibration or makes Lara support a truth premise.

## Which soundness contracts are checked?

The derivation system has exactly eight independent derivation constructors: skip, pure assignment, sequence, conditional, loop, consequence, test and noninterfering parallel. `ValidTriple` independently quantifies over every rigid ghost environment and actual terminating admitted reference execution. No derivation constructor takes the desired triple's validity as a premise. `derivation_sound` follows by induction over these constructors; it proves partial correctness, not termination or empirical applicability.

A false guard means successful evaluation to `some false`, not negation of successful evaluation to `some true`. Undefined guards satisfy neither branch assertion. Pure assignment includes typed value and dataset assignment, but excludes test: computing a p-value does not append evidence. Test uses the complete primitive preimage, including its report and actual dataset-value/test ledger delta.

Statistical framing is not limited to the `StructuralFrameTerm` fragment. Fresh-read certificates cover the full typed term language, nested knowledge, outer binders and fixed world/view scopes. Knowledge transport uses actual admitted command forth/back alternatives. Whole ambient history and the written report are not silently framed. Dataset aliases are mathematical equality, not distinct-name assumptions.

The five corrected statistical rules (`twoHT`, `lowHT`, `upHT`, `multDisj`, `multConj`) are applications of the test constructor followed by consequence. Bookkeeping is proved first; only then is the stored observation turned into statistical belief. The single-rule scientific binding equates the null formula with the actual null hypothesis and binds the conditional statistic push-forward to the declared null law. The pair binding names the selected coupling, actual conditional joint statistic law, both statistic marginals and both dataset-population marginals. The latter two obligations are required because statistic push-forwards alone need not determine population marginals.

Composite disjunction uses the unclamped sum and conjunction uses the minimum. A first belief does not supply a stored-report equality or license framing that belief through a second test. Numeric availability, scientific applicability, conditional procedure validity and artifact support remain separate.

## Which completeness contracts are checked?

`weakestLiberalPrecondition` is independently defined over all terminating public executions, not over derivations. `relative_completeness` reconstructs a derivation from `ValidTriple` and source `WellFormed` in the exact fixed context. `controlLogWlpFamily` supplies the proved finite assertion representative; no uninstantiated expressiveness premise remains in the final theorem. There is no implication decision procedure, termination theorem or automatic proof-search claim.

The reconstruction follows [Appendix B.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#A2.SS5), with the richer ghost-sort correction rather than the impossible general-real integer encoding. A representative has four fixed outer binders: source world, program-state trace, separate metadata seed world and metadata trace. The two integer metadata cells record only finite control location and cumulative primitive-command count. They occupy the separate ghost world, not reserved program memory. Actual real, population, product, list, dataset and state values keep their typed carriers.

The body expands the finite residual graph into local guard and primitive equations under one outer bounded integer binder. It checks full source/ambient view equality, source and program-prefix admission, initial/final locations, initial count zero, final count equal to program-trace length, and bounded counts at every edge. Guards require successful Boolean evaluation and preserve the count; primitives require successful expanded-view evaluation and increment it once. There is no quantifier under `K`, opaque run/WLP atom, interpreter callback or run-length-dependent binder family.

`ControlLog.encode` follows actual finite steps: administrative guards append metadata only, while each primitive appends its literal command state. `ControlLog.decode` replays a locally valid ghost log from the actual admitted source. Arbitrary ghost states need not be literal command states. Replay proves equality of the full terminal semantic view: visible memory/datasets, complete history, actual hidden memory, compatible-hidden set and provenance. Scoped satisfaction and capture-avoiding weakening then preserve arbitrary modal and outer-quantified posts in the unchanged original environment.

Canonical reconstruction covers every primitive and program constructor. Sequence instantiates the first induction hypothesis with the second child's representative. Loop uses its own representative as invariant, the body hypothesis with that invariant, and successful `some false` exit. Parallel uses explicit noninterference and the universal sequential/parallel outcome correspondence. An illegal source has true liberal precondition because public execution requires `WellFormed`; `malformed_program_not_derivable` nevertheless rejects derivation. Vacuous validity is not sufficient for completeness without source legality.

## Which finite statistical contracts are checked?

The finite instance uses a normalized rational `FiniteProbability.Law`, not a caller-supplied probability measure or calibration flag. ## What does the proved relative-completeness result require?

[Theorem 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S8.SS3) is relative to true implications of the assertion theory used by Conseq. The paper's universal-outcome transformer, called `wp` in [Appendix B.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#A2.SS5), is a weakest **liberal** precondition: `WLP(C,Q)(w) := ∀u, executes C w u → satisfies u Q`. A program with no terminating outcomes satisfies it vacuously. Neither relative completeness nor the Loop rule proves termination, and [§8.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S8.SS4) explicitly retains undecidability.

`WlpAssertionFamily` names an independent finite-assertion representation contract for structural proof reconstruction. `controlLogWlpFamily` discharges it: the formula is built from the frozen legal constructors, actual typed world/trace ghosts, a bounded outer integer binder and finite local edge equations. `ControlLog.encode`, `ControlLog.decode`, scoped satisfaction and full-view congruence prove the uniform correspondence for every exact context and original ghost environment. The final `relative_completeness` has no assumed family or expressiveness premise. Its premises are `ValidTriple` and source `WellFormed`; relativity remains the existing consequence rule's true, exact-context `AssertionEntails`. Neither a shallow WLP predicate nor a finite truth table replaces the representation.

Actual-world substitution under `K` fails even with rigid equality. Let `w` and `u` have the same visible trace and different hidden values `x=0` and `x=1`. At `w`, `K(x=0)` is false because of `u`. Replacing `x` with `w(x)` makes the formula `K(0=0)`, which is true at both worlds. A concrete hidden memory describes the actual state; it does not describe the agent's information set. Thus the loop representation must retain how execution transforms every accessible alternative. This obstacle follows directly from the [knowledge clause](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S6.SS2.SSS1); it is not resolved by changing ordinary predicate stability or choosing Mathlib.

The artifact fragment has an independent meaning before any bridge checker accepts it. Certified inputs also bind the evidence snapshot, manifest and registry. Equality of labels or identically spelled prose is insufficient: transport needs exact equality or a proved interpretation-preserving map for the actual carriers.

The reference contract distinguishes a procedure-wide conditional assertion from a single modeled application and from defeasible warrant at a source snapshot. It derives a postcondition only from actual precondition satisfaction and the exact run. Applicability evidence can support a conditional method claim while leaving assumptions unresolved. Natural-language interpretation fidelity, physical sampling fidelity and external-log completeness are obligations outside the checked model, as the paper's [§10](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S10) already distinguishes formal reasoning from external justification.

## Why is the metatheory reconstructed in Lean?

The author-linked [StatWhy](https://github.com/fm4stats/statwhy) library is BHL-based Why3 software. Its recorded SMT and Coq sessions prove store-based belief and ELHT lemmas relative to declared interpretation axioms, and its statistical subroutines are assumed contracts. It contains no reference program syntax, small-step semantics, inductive Hoare derivation relation or general soundness/relative-completeness proof. Its unrestricted interpretation axiom requires `i(Real(Const r)) = r` for every function `i`, which is inconsistent under ordinary function/real semantics, and its statistical belief omits the reference's `¬varpi` branch.

MIT source reuse with attribution can supply examples or proof ideas. Lara must reprove any reused statement against its own explicit definitions and corrected hypotheses. Foreign SMT success or Coq `Qed` cannot enter a Lean theorem dependency chain by citation. Reuse as a checked dependency would require a checked translation of the proof terms and every transitive assumption, plus representation/semantic-preservation proofs for typed values, worlds/observations, canonical history, formula binding, execution and derivations. No such compatible chain was located, so the BHL metatheory, concrete instance and bridge correspondence are reconstructed in Lean.

## Which scientific assumptions stay outside the proof?

A Lean proof establishes a statement inside the bound mathematical model. It does not show that the claim's prose faithfully describes the selected hypothesis or that the modeled population describes a physical population. The reference's [§10.1-10.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07074v3#S10) separates statistical requirements from external empirical justification. Lara must retain that separation even when it has checked applicability evidence and a complete modeled history.
